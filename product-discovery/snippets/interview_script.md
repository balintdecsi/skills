# Customer-Discovery Interview — script and synthesis

Based on the Mom Test (Rob Fitzpatrick): talk about their life, not your idea. Aim for
5–10 interviews per segment; 20–40 minutes each. Two people if possible: one asks, one
writes verbatim quotes.

Store each interview as `docs/discovery/interviews/YYYY-MM-DD_pNN.md`. Use a pseudonym
(P01, P02…); keep names and contact details outside the repo.

## Script

**Open (2 min)** — "I'm researching how people handle <area>. I'm not selling anything;
there are no wrong answers. Mind if I take notes?"

**Context (5 min)**
- "Tell me about your role / your week when it comes to <area>."
- "What are the most annoying parts of <activity>?"

**The last time (10–15 min)** — dig into one concrete, recent episode
- "Tell me about the last time you <had the problem>. What happened?"
- "What did you do about it? What tools, people, workarounds?"
- "What did that cost you — time, money, stress? Who else was affected?"
- "What have you tried before? Why did you stop using it?"
- "How often does this happen?"

**Priorities (5 min)**
- "Where does this rank against the other things you're dealing with?"
- "If you could wave a magic wand, what would be different?" (listen for the outcome,
  not feature requests)

**Money and decisions (5 min)**
- "Do you currently pay for anything to deal with this? How much?"
- "Who decides on buying tools for this? Who signs off on the budget?"

**Close (2 min)**
- "Who else should I talk to about this?" (ask for an intro)
- The commitment ask: "Can I show you an early version in two weeks?" /
  "We're opening a paid pilot at <price> — want a spot?"

**Never ask**: "Would you use…?", "Would you buy…?", "How much would you pay?",
"Do you like the idea?". If they compliment the idea, redirect: "Thanks — how do you deal
with it today?"
